⚙️ How AutoJobApply Works: Architecture & Pipeline
Local agentic discovery, full description enrichment, and intelligent candidate fit ranking
🌐
1. Multi-Platform Scraping

Discovers vacancies across Internshala (freshers, junior developer vacancies across India) and LinkedIn (global job search endpoint) matching your target role and location.

📑
2. Detail Scraping & Enrichment

For every job card discovered, AutoJobApply fetches the live detail page to extract the full description, required technical stack, and responsibilities.

🛡️
3. Deduplication Registry

Maintains a persistent ledger (seen_jobs.json). You never waste AI tokens re-evaluating or re-reading positions you already examined.

👤
4. Candidate Profiling

Extracts your skills, seniority level, and previous roles from your resume in documents/cv/ into a structured JSON benchmark (profile.json).

🧠
5. NVIDIA NIM Fit Scoring

Llama 3.2 grades each role from 0 to 100, strictly enforcing hard deal-breakers (e.g. compensation, language), applying score boosts, and identifying exact skill gaps.

🎯
6. Human-in-the-Loop Apply

Automated bot application submissions are dangerous and lead to portal blacklists. AutoJobApply sorts jobs by match score and lets you review AI reasoning before manually applying in 1 click.

💡 Platform Coverage & FAQs
Understanding platform differences, bot protection, and tips for best results
Why Internshala alongside LinkedIn?
Internshala provides fast, server-side rendered listings with 0 captchas and direct access to hundreds of active junior and fresher full-stack developer roles in India and Remote.
What about Naukri?
Naukri protects its web search endpoints with Akamai/Cloudflare bot shielding (HTTP 406: recaptcha required). Direct HTTP requests get blocked unless authenticated with active browser session cookies. Internshala and LinkedIn provide unblocked public access.
Is my personal data or API key safe?
Yes! Everything runs 100% locally on your machine. Your API key is stored in .env and your resume is stored in documents/cv/—both are strictly git-ignored and never shared.
How do I customize the ranking rules?
Go to the Rubric & AI Model Settings tab to edit hard deal-breakers (lines that trigger a 0 score) and score bonuses (e.g. +5 for remote-first or AI companies).
